# Setting

## Decided

### Era and Earth

- **Near future**, roughly 2080 to 2150: the first permanent lunar industry. (B1)
- **Earth is close and in charge**: supplies, orders and a short signal delay. (B2)
- **A patchwork:** each base runs itself. (B3)
- **Why people are on the Moon:** resources and research. Destinations farther out
  are common knowledge in the lore, but the player can't reach them yet. (B5)

### History

- **The abandoned facility** is a base from an earlier era of rivalry, even open
  conflict, between corporations. That is why its defences still fight. (B6)
- **Dumped hardware** lies on the surface not far from the hub. (B7)

### The exotic material

- **What it is:** traces of a solid material in the regolith. Hard to extract. What
  is left now are remnants: the corporations took most of it. (B8)
- **What it does:** unreasonably good radiation protection. Later (after 1.0) this
  unlocks compact, safe fission power sources for the player, for heavier suits and
  more. (B8)
- **Who knows:** everyone, locally. It's mined, sold and used, and nobody asks. (B9)
- **The excavation site** is a facility that filters regolith for the material and
  accumulates it. (B10)
- **First hint:** the orbital station's power core uses it. A shielded fission core
  as portable as a large petrol generator, with far more power for its volume and
  weight. (B11)

## Proposed

Who runs each place (B4), waiting for the user:

| Place | Run by |
|---|---|
| Surface hub | An independent settlers' co-op that rents space to everyone |
| Mining base | An Earth mining company, under contract |
| Research outpost | An Earth agency or a university consortium |
| Orbital station | A transport company, the port authority for traffic to and from Earth |
| Excavation site | The company that won the old rivalry and now holds the claim |
| Abandoned facility | Nobody now. It belonged to the side that lost |

## Open

1. Earth in charge yet each base runs itself: Earth owns, bases set their own rules
   within contracts, and Earth acts through orders, shipments and audits? (B2, B3)
2. How long ago was the rivalry: a generation, 10 to 15 years (veterans still around),
   or a cold peace now? (B6)
3. Was it fought over the material? Are both sides still present? (B6)
4. The defences: military hardware left on watch, or industrial security turned
   deadly? (B6)
5. "Accumulation": does the site concentrate traces, or does the material slowly
   **grow back** in the regolith, which nobody can explain? (B10)
6. What exactly can't be explained about the material? A second oddity as a hint
   for later? (B8)
7. Its names: a miners' word and a formal or company name. (B8)
8. What the player can do with it in 1.0 (proposed: a modification giving strong
   radiation resistance; bulk amounts wait for reactors). (B8)
9. Where the radiation comes from: solar storms, wartime reactors or weapons at the
   facility, or both. (B8)
10. What makes the excavation field a hazard maze: machinery, dust, radiation, or
    the material itself? (A4, B10)
11. Is the shielded core well-known (old, rare, costly) or new and prestigious? Can
    the player see it in 1.0? (B11)
12. Threat mix: environment, machines, people, the strange. (B12)
13. Off-limits themes: aliens, AI minds, psychics, the evil-corporation cliché,
    military space opera. (B13)
14. How hard the science is: gravity, the long lunar day and night, signal delay,
    real units. (B14)
15. Real lunar places, invented ones, or both. (B15)
16. Naming style for people and places. (B16)
17. Reference works for the feel. (B17)
