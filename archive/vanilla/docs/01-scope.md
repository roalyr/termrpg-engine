# Scope

## Decided

- **Purpose.** The opening chapter of the open-ended game, built to be extended by
  later packs or versions. It should still feel like a whole experience. (A1)
- **Length.** One playthrough of the content takes 2 to 4 hours. (A2)
- **Focus: exploration and spatial progression.** Progress means reaching new places
  and surviving new kinds of terrain; new ways to travel follow from those. Building,
  fighting and crafting are the means of getting farther. (A3)
- **What blocks the way outward,** in order: hazards the suit can't handle and frame
  size or capability first; enemies second; permissions rarely. (A3)
- **Combat is occasional and dangerous:** guardians at thresholds, not a constant.
  Later packs may make it more common. The world goes from normal to strange and
  unusual. (A3)
- **Starting point:** the Moon. Nothing is reused from the old game. (A4, A6)
- **Pack id:** `vanilla` (`packs/vanilla`). (A5)
- **Not in scope:** presets (backstories at character creation).

## Done for 1.0

- The player can reach the farthest point of interest on the surface.
- Out of habitats and safe zones the player has relative autonomy: a good enough
  build lets them stay out and travel without running back for every need.
- Each step outward needs a build upgrade.
