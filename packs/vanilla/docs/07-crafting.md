# Craft and salvage

## Decided

- The exotic material is extracted at the excavation site and is the key to progress
  after 1.0. (A4.8, B8)
- The orbital station sells imported parts, including parts to expand the player's
  workshop or room. (A4.7)
- Dumped hardware near the hub is an early source of salvage. (B7)

## Open

1. Where most materials come from. (G1)
2. Workshops: how many, where, which services and grades. (G2)
3. Which designs can be learned; which items are unique. (G3)
4. How much repair matters. (G4)
5. What the exotic modification does that nothing else can. (G5, B8)
